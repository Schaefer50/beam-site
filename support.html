Support

How can we help?

BeamOS plays your own Plex, Jellyfin and Emby servers plus Plex Live TV and your Xtream Codes IPTV subscription, on Apple TV, iPhone and iPad, playing files untouched wherever the device can handle them.

Getting help

Send a report from the app Settings › About › Send Feedback shows a QR code. Scan it with your phone to send BeamOS's diagnostics, without passwords or sign-in tokens. Report a Problem on the forum Search whether someone has seen it already, or start a new topic with the title, your server and what happened. Suggest a feature Share ideas and vote on what comes next. Email lschaefer50@gmail.com

Sending a report from BeamOS

  1. On your Apple TV, open BeamOS and go to Settings › About › Send Feedback.
  2. Scan the QR code with your iPhone's camera. The report opens on your phone, already filled in.
  3. Add what you were doing and send it.

TestFlight testers: use TestFlight's Send Beta Feedback too. If BeamOS crashed, open it again first, then send feedback saying what you were doing.

Before you write

  • Live TV supports Plex Live TV & DVR and Xtream Codes accounts. M3U playlists aren't supported yet.
  • Type your server's address; automatic discovery isn't built yet.
  • Jellyfin and Emby sign in with a username and password. Quick Connect and SSO aren't supported yet.

Subscriptions

  • Restore a purchase: Settings › About › Restore Purchases in BeamOS.
  • Cancel or change a plan: on your Apple TV, Settings › Users and Accounts › your account › Subscriptions.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
